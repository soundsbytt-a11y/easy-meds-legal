Privacy Policy & Medical Disclaimer

Updated August 20, 2026

Educational use only

This app is designed strictly for educational study and reference purposes. It should not be used to replace professional medical judgment or direct patient care.

Medical disclaimer

Easy Meds is a nursing pharmacology study aid. It does not diagnose, treat, prescribe, calculate patient-specific doses, or provide clinical decision support. Medication information may be incomplete, simplified, outdated, or unsuitable for a particular patient.

Always verify information using current official drug references, institutional policy, the medication order, and a qualified healthcare professional. Never delay emergency care or make a patient-care decision based only on this app. Contact local emergency services for an emergency.

Data collection

Easy Meds does not require an account and does not transmit personal information or study activity to developer-operated servers. The current version contains no third-party advertising or analytics SDKs, does not access HealthKit, and does not collect patient health information.

Easy Meds does not sell, rent, or share user information.

Android waitlist

If you choose to join the Android waitlist, we collect the email address you provide and, if supplied, your first name through Google Forms. Responses may be stored in a Google Sheet associated with Easy Meds.

We use this information only to send Android launch and availability updates that you agreed to receive. Joining the waitlist is optional. Easy Meds does not sell this information.

Google processes the form responses as a service provider. You can review Google’s Privacy Policy. You may unsubscribe or request deletion of your waitlist information at any time by emailing easymedsadmin@gmail.com.

Information stored on your device

The app stores your nickname and appearance preference; favourites, XP, streaks, achievements, and lesson progress; and an optional profile photo selected by you. This information is stored locally and is not uploaded by Easy Meds.

Subscriptions and payments

Easy Meds subscriptions are purchased and managed through Apple’s App Store using StoreKit. Apple processes payment and billing information. Easy Meds does not receive or store your full payment-card details.

The app receives transaction and subscription-entitlement information from Apple only as needed to unlock paid access and restore purchases. Free trials and subscriptions automatically renew unless cancelled at least 24 hours before the end of the current period. You can manage or cancel them in your Apple Account subscription settings.

Profile photos

If you choose a profile photo, Apple’s photo picker gives Easy Meds access only to the image you select. The app creates a resized copy and stores it locally. Easy Meds does not upload the photo. You can remove it from the Profile screen.

Retention and deletion

Local preferences and progress remain on the device until you change them, reset them through available app controls, or delete the app. Removing Easy Meds deletes its locally stored app data, subject to the device’s Apple-managed backup and restore settings.

Because Easy Meds does not operate a user-account server, there is no server-side account or personal-data record to delete.

Sources and methodology

Medication concepts were selected and summarized from instructor-provided PNM 300 pharmacology lecture materials (2026), covering CNS, cardiovascular, renal, gastrointestinal, endocrine, pain, antimicrobial, and respiratory medications.

Principal textbook identified in the course materials:
Sealock, K., Seneviratne, L., Lilley, L. L., & Snyder, J. S. (2020). Pharmacology for Canadian Healthcare Practice (4th Canadian ed.). Elsevier.

The source material was reorganized into short lessons, medication summaries, flashcards, and quiz questions for study. Easy Meds is not affiliated with or endorsed by the course institution, the textbook authors, or Elsevier.

Accuracy and updates

Pharmacology guidance, product information, and institutional policies change. Easy Meds does not guarantee that every entry is complete, current, or error-free. Users should compare study content with current product monographs, authoritative references, and their institution’s policies.

Children’s privacy

Easy Meds is intended for nursing learners and is not directed to children under 13. The app does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Contact and policy changes

For privacy, content, or support questions, email easymedsadmin@gmail.com. This policy may be updated when app features or data practices change.
